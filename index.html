About Publications CV

Welcome to my page!

I am a researcher at the Communication systems Department (CoS) at KTH-Royal Institute of Technology. I am vice-director of the Swedish Wireless Innovation Network (SweWIN) research center and a voting member at the Swedish national committee for AI standardization (SIS/TK 421). I completed my masters at Telecom Paris Tech and KTH, and my PhD at INPT Rabat.

My research is in the area of digital governance, with a focus on several aspects including AI Governance, AI in telecommunications, sociotechnology, linguistics and Internet of Things.
